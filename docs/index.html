Privacy Policy

Kaarya Siddhi · Last updated: 07 July 2026

Kaarya Siddhi (“the App”, “we”, “us”, “our”) is a task management app built for Railway staff to assign, track, and manage day-to-day work. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and the choices available to you.

By creating an account and using Kaarya Siddhi, you agree to the collection and use of your information as described below.

1. Information We Collect

a) Information you provide directly

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Mobile number
  • Department and designation
  • Profile picture
  • Task-related content you create or upload, such as task titles, descriptions, attachments, comments, and extension requests

b) Information generated as you use the App

  • Task assignments, statuses, priorities, and deadlines
  • Task submissions and reviews
  • Chat messages sent within the App
  • Notifications and their read/unread status
  • Connections between employees and administrators or workspaces

c) Information collected automatically

  • Login timestamps and session activity
  • Basic device and app version details, used for troubleshooting and security

We do not collect precise location data.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Assign, track, and display tasks relevant to your role
  • Send One-Time Passwords (OTPs) for login verification via email
  • Deliver in-app notifications relevant to your tasks and requests
  • Enable communication between connected employees and administrators
  • Generate task reports and summaries for administrators
  • Keep the App secure and functioning properly

We do not sell your personal information to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. How Your Information Is Stored and Secured

Your information is stored using Supabase, our cloud database provider. File attachments uploaded within the App are stored using Cloudinary, and OTP verification emails are sent through Brevo, our email delivery provider.

We take reasonable technical and organizational steps to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. That said, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Third-Party Services

Kaarya Siddhi relies on the following third-party services to operate:

  • Supabase — database and backend infrastructure
  • Cloudinary — file and attachment storage
  • Brevo — transactional email delivery (OTP verification)

These providers process your information only to deliver the services listed above, under their own respective privacy and security practices.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account and task-related information for as long as your account stays active, or as long as needed to provide the App's functionality. If you request account deletion, we will remove your personal information within a reasonable time, except where retention is required for legitimate administrative, legal, or record-keeping purposes.

6. Your Rights and Choices

You may:

  • Access and review the personal information linked to your account
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your account and associated personal information
  • Withdraw consent to non-essential notifications through the App's settings

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details in Section 9.

7. Children's Privacy

Kaarya Siddhi is built for working professionals and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the age of 18.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal, operational, or regulatory reasons. We will notify users of material changes through the App. Continued use of the App after any changes means you accept the updated policy.
